Bio

Hi! My name is Olivia Johnson. I'm an active and outdoorsy extrovert, who loves playing soccer, planning adventures, and trying new things (hopefully with lots of friends). My personal mission is to enable others to achieve their full potential.

After nearly a decade working in People Ops and Learning & Development in tech startups, I've seen what engaged and successful individuals have in common; the confidence and ability to be self starters, think critically, and experiment creatively. With all the rapid changes occurring in the world, I want to move upstream and help guide the next generation so they are prepared to handle whatever comes their way.

I am a facilitator at heart, and have run engaging learning experiences across corporate, elementary classroom, and summer camp settings. Having built a successful career in small, fast-paced tech startups, I've also honed my ability to quickly learn new skills on the job, figure out how to maximize experiences with limited resources, and build positive and collaborative relationships across teams.

In the next phase of my career, I want to build upon my current skills and learn a new craft in pedagogy within a school that nurtures proactivity, critical thinking, and experimentation in the next generation. I'm excited to help elementary students imagine and then forge a path toward their full potential. If you are hiring for a position like this, I would love to chat!
